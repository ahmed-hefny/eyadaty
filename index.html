Questions

Frequently asked

Can my nurse see diagnoses or prescriptions?

No. Nurses manage patients, visits and the queue only. Chief complaint, diagnosis, doctor notes, prescriptions, follow-up details and General Notes are doctor-only, and the server enforces it.

How does payment work?

Choose a plan and send us your details. Our team contacts you, payment is handled directly, and we activate your subscription. Online payment is coming later.

What does "1 month free" mean on Basic?

Basic starts with a free month. It's also added to paid terms: a 6-month purchase gives you 7 months, and a 1-year purchase gives you 13 months.

What happens when my subscription ends?

Access for you and your nurses pauses until you renew. All of your data is preserved — nothing is deleted.

What counts toward the patient limit?

Each unique patient linked to your practice. Visits are unlimited. Removing a patient with no visits frees the slot; patients with visit history stay counted.

Can I work across more than one clinic?

Yes. Professional includes two clinics, each with its own independent queue. Reports can combine all clinics or focus on one.
